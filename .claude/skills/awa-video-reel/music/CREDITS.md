# Music credits

Starter tracks, found through Openverse and converted to 48 kHz MP3. Full details per track (creator, licence, source page, moods) are in `library.json`.

- **CC0** (public domain): no credit needed. 14 tracks, from Freesound and Wikimedia Commons.
- **CC BY**: credit the creator in the post caption. 1 track: "Got Funk" by Kevin MacLeod (CC BY 3.0). `render.sh` prints the exact line and writes `MUSIC-CREDIT.txt` when this track is used.

Add your own tracks by dropping MP3s into this folder; put the mood in the file name (for example `chill - my track.mp3`). Only add music you have the rights to use. Trending songs from Instagram or TikTok are copyrighted: add those in the app, on the `_nomusic` copy.
