Questions US owners ask us.

How does it work when you are not in the United States?

You share bank statements, invoices and receipts online. We record and reconcile everything, then send your reports each month. Questions go through WhatsApp or email, so nothing waits for a meeting.

Can you work in the software I already use?

Yes. We work in Zoho Books, Sage, QuickBooks Online, and Excel or Google Sheets. If you use something else, tell us in the health check and we will confirm before quoting.

My books are behind. Can you still help?

Yes. Catch-up is priced per month of records behind, and clean-up as a one-off project. We rebuild the missing periods, reconcile every account and hand you a clean closing position.

Do you file taxes or give tax advice?

No. We keep your books ready for your CPA or tax preparer. Tax preparation, tax and legal advice, and audit are outside our service.

How is my price decided?

Prices are shown in US dollars. Your quote depends on transaction volume, number of accounts, software, payroll scope and the condition of the books. It is confirmed in writing before work begins.
