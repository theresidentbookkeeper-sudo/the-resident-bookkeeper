About the service

Bookkeeping that fits the way your business operates.

The Resident Bookkeeper provides remote bookkeeping support to businesses in the United States. Services are designed for owners who need dependable transaction review, reconciliations, catch-up and cleanup work, accounts support and financial reporting without adding a full-time bookkeeping employee.

Core services include full-cycle bookkeeping, bank reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable support, catch-up and cleanup bookkeeping, and financial reporting.

Questions

Common bookkeeping questions

Can you work with my existing accounting software?

We can scope support around the accounting system and records you already use. Common systems in our workflow include QuickBooks Online, Zoho Books, Sage, Excel/Google Sheets and other accounting platforms.

Can you take on books that are behind?

Yes. Catch-up and cleanup work is available for records that need historical transaction entry, reconciliation, classification review and organization.

Do you provide tax or legal advice?

Tax preparation or filing, tax/legal advice, audit or attestation, investment advice and other excluded services are not part of standard bookkeeping packages.

How do I start?

Complete the Free Books Health Check or send an enquiry through the main website. We review the scope before work begins.
