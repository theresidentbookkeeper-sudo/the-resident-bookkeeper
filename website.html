Clear scope

Know exactly what your bookkeeping support includes.

Every engagement is scoped clearly so you know what we will handle, what we may need from you, and what falls outside the bookkeeping service.

What's included

  • Transaction review — Categorize and review income, expenses, transfers and unusual or unclear activity.
  • Reconciliation — Reconcile included bank and credit-card accounts and investigate differences.
  • Receipts & support — Request supporting documents when transactions are unclear, unusual or material.
  • Quality control — Review duplicates, uncategorized items, missing information and obvious bookkeeping issues.
  • Reporting — Monthly P&L and Balance Sheet review, plus a summary of notable items and client questions.
  • Client communication — Clarification requests, follow-ups and bookkeeping status updates.

Optional services

  • Catch-up / cleanup work beyond the monthly package
  • Additional transaction volume
  • Invoicing and bill tracking
  • Payroll support and payroll record preparation
  • Tax-ready records / year-end schedules for your tax professional
  • Additional administrative bookkeeping services

Optional services are quoted separately based on scope.

Not included

  • Tax preparation or filing on your behalf
  • Tax or legal advice
  • CPA / accounting advisory services
  • Audit or attestation
  • Payroll tax filing or statutory tax remittance on your behalf
  • Investment advice
  • Moving client funds or paying bills without separate authorization
